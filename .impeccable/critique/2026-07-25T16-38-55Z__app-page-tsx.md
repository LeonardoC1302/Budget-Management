## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment. The home page could be reskinned onto any competent Tailwind personal-finance side project without changing a single component. The word "Perch" does not appear anywhere on the route — identity lives only in the browser tab title. The Night Perch North Star, the tagline, the bird motif, the multi-currency USD-normalization story, and the ambient-depth doctrine are all absent from what the user sees. Semantic color is disciplined but never composed. Summary tiles, insight cards, budget rows, and goal cards share identical .surface grammar with no shadow — nothing reads as "the perch" versus instrumentation. The single first-viewport verb is Sign out.

## Overall Impression

Competent codebase, coherent tokens, genuinely restrained analytics atoms — and a generic home page. The biggest opportunity is not to add features but to let Perch be Perch on its own front page: give the surface an identity moment, demote Sign out, break the tonal uniformity, and put reassurance where the emotional valleys are.

## What's Working

1. Analytics atoms have real restraint (SavingsLineChart, CategoryDonut).
2. Semantic color discipline is consistent across Amount, ProgressBar, DeltaPill, chart nodes.
3. Mobile primary action (bottom-anchored + Add transaction) matches the actual usage scene.

## Priority Issues

[P0] The home page carries none of Perch's identity. No wordmark, no bird, no tagline, no atmosphere. H1 is "This month" — a time-scope, not a route identity. Fix: real masthead with mark + first-person time/scope caption ("Tuesday evening, July - all figures in USD"), one signature detail (hairline horizon, low-contrast constellation), bring icon.tsx motif onto the surface. Suggested: /impeccable shape then /impeccable bolder.

[P0] Sign out is the loudest verb on the landing surface. Top-right ghost button with no confirm sits where every mobile OS trains users to expect a profile menu. Fix: wrap in account menu with ConfirmDialog on Sign out; free the slot for month picker / detail toggle / USD-freshness pill. Suggested: /impeccable clarify.

[P1] Visual hierarchy is uniform — nothing is "the perch." All .surface cards share identical fill/border/radius/padding with no shadow. Ambient-depth doctrine chosen in document is not yet in CSS. Fix: implement ambient shadow on .surface; give Balance tile a distinctive treatment. Suggested: /impeccable layout + /impeccable delight on balance.

[P1] Reassurance is missing at every emotional valley. Over-budget = red number, negative net = red delta, empty states = three grey apologies. Fix: over-state copy on BudgetRow; second-line quiet copy on negative-net DeltaPill; numbered onboarding ladder for empty states. Suggested: /impeccable onboard + /impeccable clarify.

[P2] Progressive disclosure entirely absent. Every section fully expanded; cognitive-load fails 6 of 8. Fix: collapse Budgets/Goals to top item + "See more", or invert stack. Suggested: /impeccable distill.

[P3] Bottom-nav icon set unreadable. Bespoke glyphs on 8 destinations; mobile shows only active icon. Fix: real line-icon set; group 4 primary + 4 secondary. Suggested: /impeccable polish + /impeccable distill.
